# AI Amigos external method-review checklist v0.1.1

Use the release manifest to download the exact artifact and verify its SHA-256 checksum before review.

## Reviewer record

- Reviewer name and relevant qualification
- Organization and role, if the reviewer chooses to disclose them
- Review scope and exclusions
- Relationship to AI Amigos or the author
- Compensation, funding, collaboration, reporting-line, or other conflict disclosure
- Exact method version, release ID, artifact path, and SHA-256 checksum
- Review date and independently controlled verification URL

## Decision

Choose one: approve within stated scope; changes required; reject; withdraw a prior decision.

The decision must explain the evidence inspected, tests performed, limitations, disagreements, and any claim that must not be made. A reviewer may correct or withdraw a decision by publishing a dated notice at the same reviewer-controlled URL and notifying AI Amigos.

## Independence boundary

AI Amigos may link to a reviewer-controlled record but must not write, backdate, or silently revise it. Payment, if any, must be disclosed and does not make a review independent. A review of structure is not proof of adoption, outcomes, originality, major significance, or immigration eligibility.
