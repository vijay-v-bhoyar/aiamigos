<p><strong>What are the different types of GPT models?</strong></p>



<p>There are several different types of models, including:</p>

<li><strong>GPT-J:</strong>&nbsp;A 178B parameter model that is similar to GPT-3,&nbsp;but with some improvements in accuracy and performance.</li>



<li><strong>GPT-Neo:</strong>&nbsp;A 1.3B parameter model that is a smaller and faster version of GPT-3.&nbsp;GPT-Neo is a good choice for users who need a GPT model that can run on less powerful hardware.</li>



<li><strong>Megatron-Turing NLG:</strong> A 530B parameter model that is one of the largest language models ever created. Megatron-Turing NLG is still under development, but it has shown promising results in a variety of tasks.</li>

</ul>
