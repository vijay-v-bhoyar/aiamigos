<p><strong>What is quantum AI?</strong></p>



<p>Quantum AI, also known as quantum computing with artificial intelligence, is a field that combines the principles of quantum mechanics with artificial intelligence to develop new algorithms and techniques for solving complex problems. Quantum AI has the potential to revolutionize a variety of industries, including healthcare, finance, and manufacturing.</p>







<p><strong>How does quantum AI work?</strong></p>



<p>Quantum AI works by harnessing the unique properties of quantum mechanics, such as superposition and entanglement, to perform computations that are impossible for classical computers. In superposition, a qubit can be in a state of 0 and 1 at the same time, which allows for parallel processing of information. Entanglement is the phenomenon where two or more qubits are linked together in such a way that they can share information instantaneously, regardless of their distance apart.</p>
